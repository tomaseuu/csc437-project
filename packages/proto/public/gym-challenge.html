Challenge

Gym Showdown Challenge

A 30-day habit challenge between two friends focused on gym goals.

Challenges Gym Mindfulness
T Thomas

Challenge Details

Duration: 30 days

Status: Active

Reward: The loser buys the winner a PopMart figure.

Participants

  • User A with the habit Gym 5 Times Per Week
  • User B with a goal of going to the gym 3 times per week

How Scoring Works

Each participant logs activity during the week. If they meet their personal weekly goal, they earn 1 point for that week.
