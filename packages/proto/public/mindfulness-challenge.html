Mindfulness Duel Challenge

A weekly habit challenge where two friends compete using different self-improvement routines.

Challenges Gym Mindfulness

4 weeks Active Punishment: The loser gets pied by the winner. User A with a goal of meditating daily User B with a goal of journaling daily Both users log once per day. At the end of each week, they either pass or fail their goal for that week.
