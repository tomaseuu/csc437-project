Challenge

Mindfulness Duel Challenge

A weekly habit challenge where two friends compete using different self-improvement routines.

Challenges Gym Mindfulness
T Thomas

Challenge Details

Duration: 4 weeks

Status: Active

Punishment: The loser gets pied by the winner.

Participants

  • User A with a goal of meditating daily
  • User B with a goal of journaling daily

How Scoring Works

Both users log once per day. At the end of each week, they either pass or fail their goal for that week.
